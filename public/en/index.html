Traditional Chinese Medicine + Western Medicine

Zhu’s Scalp Acupuncture was established by Professor Ming Qing Zhu, with Moyee (Peng Zhiyun / 彭芝芸) as co-founder. Its clinical tradition brings classical Chinese medical thinking into dialogue with modern Western medicine.
