From Asia to the World

Clinical knowledge crossing borders.

Rooted in clinical practice and academic exchange in Asia, Zhu’s Scalp Acupuncture progressively entered a broader international medical and educational community—from Japan and Malaysia to Europe, Australia and beyond.

This journey represents more than the spread of an acupuncture technique. It is an ongoing dialogue among clinical practice, functional recovery, Traditional Chinese Medicine, Western medicine and professional education.
