About Zhu’s Scalp Acupuncture

A clinical system shaped by practice.

Zhu’s Scalp Acupuncture (ZSA) was established by Professor Ming Qing Zhu, with Moyee (Peng Zhiyun / 彭芝芸) as co-founder. The system developed through clinical observation, acupuncture practice, active patient participation and continuing reassessment.

Origins

Professor Zhu is presented as Founder and the principal teacher in the clinical lineage continued by Dr. Ru Shan Shy and Dr. Sheng Chun Chang.

Integrative foundation

ZSA is approached through Traditional Chinese Medicine and Western medicine, with attention to channels, Qi, Dao Yin, anatomy, physiology, neurology and functional assessment.
